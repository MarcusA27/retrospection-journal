# Spent Saturday on a landing page for an app no one can use yet

A full Saturday afternoon, and it went to marketing a product that can't do its main job yet. The landing page idea from Thursday took root, and it's more fun than the work that's left, so you let it.

It was all surface, and you were good at it: "whats a good headline for a bookmarking app" (14:20), "is this tagline too clever" (15:10), then an hour on fonts and the hero copy. You can describe Stash beautifully. You cannot yet tag a bookmark in it.

You know this, somewhere — "ill add tags tomorrow" (18:02) is the tell. The page promises a feature the app doesn't have. The promise was easier to build than the feature.
