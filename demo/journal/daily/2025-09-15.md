# Three hours to deploy a thing that runs fine on my laptop

Monday night, and you fought the deploy for three hours. The app works perfectly in the one place it's allowed to live — your machine — and getting it anywhere else turned into trench warfare.

The play-by-play was misery and then relief: "why does it work locally but not on the vps" (21:30), a long fight with env vars, "502 again" (22:40), and finally the dumbest possible cause: "fixed it. it was a trailing slash" (23:55). Every developer's Tuesday, lived on a Monday.

It's live now, at a URL. The URL is in your notes and nowhere else. Stash exists on the internet and not one other person knows the address.
